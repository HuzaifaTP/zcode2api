Usage notes
· Paste a Coding Plan JWT or API Key into Accounts to join the rotation.
· Requests are distributed round-robin; when an account runs out of quota the next one is used.
· Quota and status are refreshed live on the Accounts page.
· Chat endpoint: /v1/messages (Anthropic Messages compatible).
